# DaBus roadmap

Ideas agreed on but not built yet.

## Live bus location
An admin picks one person on the trip (a passenger, a student or an admin riding from the
first pickup) to share their phone's location in real time, so everyone on the trip can see
where the bus is.

- The chosen person gets the request by email, with a link that asks their phone for
  location access. Nothing is shared until they accept, and they can stop at any time.
- Passengers see the position on the trip page and on their ticket while the trip is running.
- Sharing stops on its own when the trip is marked completed. Positions are not kept after
  the trip.

## Scanner accounts
Boarding (admin → Trips → Scan tickets) needs an admin account today. If drivers scan, give
them a scan-only role instead of full admin.

## Email passengers of a trip
Only once the updates board (admin → Updates) is not enough: an email to everyone booked on
a trip, from dabus@mail.daustgov.com. No SMS for now.
